Where we are

Kush Knight Riders won the Winners track and are already in the Finals. Ganges Grandmasters (won the Losers track), Pawn Pushers and King Killers play a round robin in the Repechage. The Repechage winner goes to the Finals.

  • The team with more points wins the match.
  • Tiebreak: bullet/blitz game between the top boards.
  • Board orders are fixed.

Chief Arbiter / Fight Preventer: Malay Mahajan
