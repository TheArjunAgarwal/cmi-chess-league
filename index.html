Format

Similar to Streamer Wars / Checkmate. Teams of 5 or 6 players play rapid matches against each other. Captains should try to set board order roughly in order of playing strength.

  1. Opening stage: three matches (all six teams play once). The 3 winning teams go to the Winners track; the 3 losing teams go to the Losers track.
  2. Round robin within each track (W1–W3 and L1–L3).
  3. Winners track: the winner goes straight to the Finals. The other two teams drop to Repechage.
  4. Losers track: the winner goes to Repechage. The other two teams are eliminated.
  5. Repechage: round robin between its 3 teams (R1–R3). The winner goes to the Finals.
  6. Finals!

Scoring

  • The team with more points wins the match.
  • Tiebreak: bullet/blitz game between the top boards.
